Interaction matrices and Lotka–Volterra

Typed matrices make explicit which object a number belongs to, and conversions between them ask for what they need.

CommunityMatrices.MatrixMetadata — Type
MatrixMetadata(; taxa = String[], state_variables, units, sign_convention, spatial_bounds,
                 temporal_bounds, derivation, uncertainty, software, licence)

The minimal metadata record proposed for deposited interaction matrices (interaction-matrix review; Terry et al. 2025): the species (taxa, in row order), what the state variables are, units, the sign convention, spatial and temporal bounds, how the entries were derived, how their uncertainty is represented, the software used and the licence. All fields are strings except taxa; unknown fields default to "unspecified".

CommunityMatrices.InteractionMatrix — Type
InteractionMatrix

Supertype of the typed interaction matrices: AlphaMatrix, Jacobian, CommunityMatrix, FluxMatrix, SignedDigraph and ProbabilisticNetwork. Use matrix for the numbers, matrix_metadata and taxa for the record, and size/nspecies for the dimension.

CommunityMatrices.AlphaMatrix — Type
AlphaMatrix(A, r; meta = MatrixMetadata())

Generalised Lotka–Volterra coefficients: dNᵢ/dt = Nᵢ (rᵢ + Σⱼ Aᵢⱼ Nⱼ), with per-capita effects A (negative diagonal for self-regulation) and intrinsic growth rates r. Other parameterisations (e.g. annual-plant λᵢ/(1 + Σⱼ αᵢⱼ Nⱼ)) must be converted first; the conversion belongs in meta.derivation.

CommunityMatrices.Jacobian — Type
Jacobian(J, equilibrium = Float64[]; meta = MatrixMetadata())

The Jacobian (population-level community matrix) of a dynamical system at an equilibrium, with that equilibrium when known. All Jacobian-based metrics accept it; the median metrics then use pulses proportional to equilibrium.

source
CommunityMatrices.Jacobian — Method
Jacobian(op::ContinuousJacobian; meta = MatrixMetadata())
ContinuousJacobian(j::Jacobian)

Convert between this package's typed Jacobian, which carries metadata, and ResilienceCore's ContinuousJacobian, on which the metrics are defined. A community that implements ResilienceCore's interface (for example an assembled food web of FoodWebDynamics.jl) gives its Jacobian as Jacobian(linearise(c)).

source
CommunityMatrices.CommunityMatrix — Type
CommunityMatrix(M, equilibrium; meta = MatrixMetadata())

Per-capita effects at an equilibrium: Mᵢⱼ = ∂(Ṅᵢ/Nᵢ)/∂Nⱼ. For Lotka–Volterra it is the coefficient matrix A. Convert with jacobian(m) = diag(N*) M and per_capita(J).

source

per_capita(j::Jacobian)

The per-capita CommunityMatrix diag(N*)⁻¹ J; needs the equilibrium, and every species present.

CommunityMatrices.FluxMatrix — Type
FluxMatrix(F, biomass; meta = MatrixMetadata())

Feeding fluxes at a steady state: F[i, j] is the rate at which consumer i removes biomass of resource j (e.g. from Ecopath or fluxweb), with the standing biomass of every species. jacobian turns it into a Jacobian under stated assumptions.

source
ResilienceCore.jacobian — Method
jacobian(a::AlphaMatrix; N = equilibrium(a))

The Jacobian of the Lotka–Volterra system a at abundances N: diag(N) A at an interior equilibrium (for other N the general form diag(r + A N) + diag(N) A is used).

source
ResilienceCore.jacobian — Method
jacobian(f::FluxMatrix; efficiency, self_regulation)

The Jacobian implied by steady-state fluxes when feeding is mass action, Fᵢⱼ = aᵢⱼ Bᵢ Bⱼ (de Ruiter et al. 1995): the effect of resource j on consumer i is eⱼ Fᵢⱼ / Bⱼ, the effect of consumer j on resource i is −Fⱼᵢ / Bⱼ, and the diagonal is −self_regulation (which fluxes cannot determine). efficiency is a vector over resources (or a scalar); self_regulation a vector (or scalar) of nonnegative rates.

source
CommunityMatrices.SignedDigraph — Type
SignedDigraph(S; meta = MatrixMetadata())
SignedDigraph(x::InteractionMatrix)

A sign pattern (entries −1, 0, +1) of the effects of species on each other, as used in loop analysis and qualitative press-perturbation analysis. The second form takes the signs of the Jacobian or community matrix of x.

source
CommunityMatrices.ProbabilisticNetwork — Type
ProbabilisticNetwork(P; meta = MatrixMetadata())

Probabilities P[i, j] that species j affects species i (for example, that i eats j), e.g. from sampling-corrected or predicted links. rand(rng, p) draws a BitMatrix of links.

source
CommunityMatrices.jacobian_metrics — Function
jacobian_metrics(j::Jacobian)

The Jacobian-based stability metrics used by Domínguez-García et al. (2019), computed by ResilienceCore in the raw sign convention of their dataset: Rinf (resilience), R0 (reactivity), Is (stochastic invariability), Amax and tmax (maximal amplification and when it is reached; (Inf, Inf) for an unstable equilibrium), S (community press sensitivity) and absumS, and, when the equilibrium is known, MRinf, MR0, MAmax, Mtmax (the median metrics, with pulses proportional to the equilibrium).

CommunityMatrices.steady_state — Function
steady_state(a::AlphaMatrix, N0; pool = findall(>(0), N0), tmax = 1e4, threshold = 1e-8,
             reseed = 1e-6, rounds = 20)

The assembled state of the Lotka–Volterra system a from N0: integrate to tmax, remove species below threshold, and solve for the survivors' interior equilibrium; while some species of the regional pool (by default those present in N0) could invade (positive invasion rate), reintroduce them at density reseed and repeat (up to rounds times). Returns (N, survivors, stable, uninvadable): the equilibrium (zeros for absent species), the survivors' indices, whether their Jacobian is stable, and whether every absent species of the pool has a negative invasion rate.

source
CommunityMatrices.glv_trajectory — Function
glv_trajectory(a::AlphaMatrix, N0, times; forcing = nothing, susceptibility = nothing,
               tstops = Float64[])

Abundances of the Lotka–Volterra system a at times from N0 at times[1], optionally with a perturbation that adds susceptibility[i] × forcing(t) to species i's per-capita growth rate (the antibiotic model of Stein et al. 2013); give the forcing's switching times as tstops. Returns a matrix with one row per time.
