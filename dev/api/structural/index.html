Structural stability and sign patterns

These analyses need only a matrix, so they apply to empirical coefficient matrices and sign patterns as well as to fitted models. The qualitative analyses are ResilienceCore's, applied to SignPattern(s).

CommunityMatrices.feasibility_domain — Function
feasibility_domain([rng], a::AlphaMatrix; samples = 100_000)

The size of the feasibility domain of a (Saavedra et al. 2017): the fraction Ω of directions of the growth-rate vector r (uniform on the unit sphere) for which the interior equilibrium −A⁻¹ r is positive, estimated from samples random directions. Returns (Ω, Ω^(1/S), se), with se the Monte Carlo standard error of Ω. Ω^(1/S) is comparable across community sizes; for A = −I it is 1/2.

source
CommunityMatrices.structural_vector — Function
structural_vector(a::AlphaMatrix)

The centroid of the feasibility cone of a: the normalised sum of the unit columns of −A (Saavedra et al. 2017). Growth-rate vectors along it are the most robustly feasible.

source
CommunityMatrices.feasibility_angle — Function
feasibility_angle(a::AlphaMatrix)

The angle in degrees between the growth-rate vector a.r and the structural_vector of a: a measure of how far the community is from the centre of its feasibility domain (larger means closer to losing feasibility).

source
ResilienceCore.SignPattern — Method
SignPattern(s::SignedDigraph)

The sign pattern of s as a ResilienceCore.SignPattern, labelled with its taxa when they are recorded. The qualitative analyses, quasi_sign_stability and press_sign_determinacy, are ResilienceCore's, applied to it.

source
ResilienceCore.quasi_sign_stability — Function
quasi_sign_stability([rng], s::SignedDigraph; samples = 10_000)

The fraction of matrices with sign pattern s and magnitudes uniform on (0, 1) that are stable (every eigenvalue with negative real part): quasi-sign-stability (Allesina & Pascual 2008). A diagonal of zeros means no self-regulation. Returns (fraction, se). ResilienceCore's method on SignPattern(s).

source
ResilienceCore.press_sign_determinacy — Function
press_sign_determinacy([rng], s::SignedDigraph; samples = 10_000)

Qualitative press-perturbation analysis (Dambacher et al. 2003; the simulation approach of QPress): draw matrices with sign pattern s and magnitudes uniform on (0, 1), keep the stable ones, and for each compute the equilibrium response to a sustained increase in each species' growth, −J⁻¹. Returns (sign, determinacy, stable, labels): the most common sign of each response (an n×n matrix; row: responding species, column: pressed species; 0 when the response is structurally zero, below 1e-10 of the largest), the fraction of stable draws with that outcome, the fraction of draws that were stable, and the taxa (or nothing). ResilienceCore's method on SignPattern(s).
